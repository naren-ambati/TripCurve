T3  terminationGracePeriodSeconds must be at least the preStop delay + the
    in-process drain + L + 1 s, where L is the declared longest_request_s, or
    else the graceful timeout of the server as a ceiling.

Vendor guidance
  Kubernetes, container lifecycle hooks (read 2026-11-12):
  "The Pod's termination grace period countdown begins before the PreStop hook
  is executed, ... This grace period applies to the total time it takes for both
  the PreStop hook to execute and for the Container to stop normally."
  https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/containers/container-lifecycle-hooks/
  gunicorn, settings reference (graceful_timeout) (read 2026-11-12):
  "Workers still alive after the timeout (starting from the receipt of the
  restart signal) are force killed."
  https://gunicorn.org/reference/settings/

When T3 does not apply or cannot decide
  - Not checked when no load balancer is found for the workload in the checked
    files.
  - NOT EVALUATED when T2 failed for the workload.
  - UNRESOLVED, never PASS or FAIL, when no ceiling is known and
    longest_request_s is not declared, or a drain or hook has a duration that
    cannot be established.
  - WARN, the worst case, when only the ceiling is known and the grace period
    does not cover it.
