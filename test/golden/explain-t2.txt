T2  A workload behind a load balancer must delay its shutdown after SIGTERM,
    with a preStop sleep, an in-process drain or a declared drain, so that
    endpoint removal can propagate before the server stops. A delay of 0 s is
    not a drain.

Vendor guidance
  AWS, EKS Best Practices, Load Balancing (read 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-02,
  research notes):
  "can be a simple Exec handler such as `sleep 10`"
  https://docs.aws.amazon.com/eks/latest/best-practices/load-balancing.html

Defaults used by T2
  - lightship-shutdown-delay-9x, lightship 9.x: 5000 ms; measured on 9.1.0;
    dated 2026-11-12; script measure/lightship-b4.mjs

When T2 does not apply or cannot decide
  - Not checked when no load balancer is found for the workload in the checked
    files.
  - UNRESOLVED, never PASS, when a drain exists but its duration cannot be
    established, or when the Node source that would show a drain is not linked
    and no preStop delay is found.
  - tripcurve checks that a delay exists, not that it is long enough; the
    propagation allowance is printed as an assumption.
